Kelpie never decides what happens next; the flow does. AI drafts the messages, and every step either side of that is fixed: same path for every lead, day or night. If any step fails, the failure pages a human. It never hides in a log.

Re-engagement: the part that compounds

The chase (this month's leads)

  • Unbooked after your grace window, Kelpie follows up: polite, branded as your business, never pushy.
  • It proposes real times and writes the confirmed visit to a shared calendar.
  • Flag any lead "leave it with me" and Kelpie never touches it.

The service-due engine (every contract, watched)

  • Every completed job logged with equipment and date; the job-done note doubles as the log entry.
  • Planned maintenance, pre-summer checks and filter cycles fall due; Kelpie reaches out ahead of time.
  • Each completed round seeds the next. Maintenance revenue stops depending on someone remembering.
What this is not. No competitor to simPRO or ServiceM8; Kelpie works in the gap they leave open. Real emergencies are never automated: a breakdown with stock at risk goes straight to your on-call tech, untouched. You own the stack, and every message is yours to read in the log.
