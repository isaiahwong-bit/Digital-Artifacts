Do I need a website and automation?

No. You can start with either. If your current site works well, we can look at the enquiry handling behind it. If you only need a website, that is fine too.

Will I need to learn technical tools?

We agree on a practical handover for the way you work. You get access to the custom work and a walkthrough. Managed care can be scoped if you want ongoing help.

What is the four-layer framework?

It is how I organise more involved automation builds: business context, instructions for each task, workflow triggers, and the interface you use. It keeps the parts easier to inspect and change. You do not need to learn the framework to start a project.

What does ownership include?

You own the custom code, content and workflows built for your project. Hosting, model usage, messaging and other third-party services may have separate running costs. Those are covered in your scope.
