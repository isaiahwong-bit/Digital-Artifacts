Before you decide

A few practical answers.

You work directly with Isaiah, from the first conversation to launch and handover.

Discuss your website↗
How long does a website take?

Standard builds usually take about 14 days from kickoff, once content and scope are ready. Larger builds take longer. Your proposal includes a timeline and review points.

Can you help with the words and images?

Yes. We shape your content, plan the pages and draft copy together where needed. You approve it before launch. Photography or other additional production is scoped separately.

Will I need automation too?

No. Every package can be a standalone website. If enquiry follow-up needs attention, we can scope that separately and connect it later.

Will the site appear on Google?

The build includes the agreed on-page search basics. Visibility also depends on competition, useful content, links and reputation. Search rankings are not guaranteed.

Who owns it, and what about later changes?

You own the custom code, domain and content. We agree on how you will manage updates at handover. Care plans are optional; individual changes can also be quoted.
