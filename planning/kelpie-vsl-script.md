# Kelpie VSL, rough script (~2:35 spoken)

Sent before any sales call, Kai Stone style: "watch this, if it's not for you tell me and
I'll cancel the call." Written to be spoken, not read. First person singular on purpose:
solo operator talking to a solo operator, even though site copy is "we". Target under
three minutes.

## Beat 1, hook (0:00-0:15, on camera)

Every tradie loses jobs to their own voicemail. You just never find out which ones.
Someone fills in your contact form at 11 at night, hears nothing back till you're off the
tools tomorrow, and books whoever answered first. You never even knew it was a race.

## Beat 2, who I am (0:15-0:30, on camera)

I'm Isaiah. I run Digital Artifacts, a one-person studio in Melbourne, and I build a thing
called Kelpie. It's a receptionist for trade businesses, except it's software. Named after
the dog for a reason: it rounds up your leads and doesn't let any wander off.

## Beat 3, what it does, two things (0:30-1:15, screen recording behind voice)

Here's what it does. One: the moment an enquiry hits your website or inbox, Kelpie answers
it. In your voice, with your services, your suburbs, your prices, from a set of files
about your business you can open and read. The customer gets a proper reply straight away,
and you get a text saying it's handled. So the job that used to sit in voicemail till
seven o'clock is answered before your boots hit the ground.

Two, and this is the part no chatbot does: it remembers everyone you've already done work
for. When a customer comes due, a year since the last hedge, a quarter since the last
treatment, Kelpie drafts them a note and checks with you before anything sends. Your old
customer list quietly turns into next month's bookings.

## Beat 4, live proof (1:15-1:45, screen: Matt's site, form in, reply out, phone buzz)

This isn't a mockup. This is Matt's. Valley Arbor, climbing arborist, here in Melbourne.
Watch: enquiry in, answer out, Matt's phone buzzes. Month one on the new site: [N] quote
requests, zero missed. And when you enquire with me at the end of this video, the reply
you get is written by the same system. I run my own studio on it. That's the demo.

## Beat 5, price out loud (1:45-2:15, on camera)

What it costs, straight up. Fifteen hundred to set up. That covers wiring Kelpie into your
website, or building you a fast one if yours is past it. Then three hundred a month. Ten
bucks a day. No contract, cancel whenever, and the files it runs on are yours, so if you
ever leave, they leave with you. I set every one of these up personally, so I take on
[two] new trades a month, capped at [twenty] all up. That's not a scarcity trick. It's one
bloke doing it properly.

## Beat 6, close (2:15-2:35, on camera)

If enquiries are already coming in and follow-up is the weak spot, Kelpie pays for itself
the first job it saves. Fill in the form below: what you do, where you work. You'll see
the whole thing run before we ever get on the phone. And if it's not for you, say so, and
that's the end of it.

## The message that sends with it

Made you a short video on exactly what Kelpie is, what it does, and what it costs. Three
minutes. If it's not for you after watching, tell me and I'll cancel the call and leave
you alone.

## Truth checks before recording (every line must survive falsification)

1. **[N]**: fill from the "DA - Valley Arbor Lead Log" Sheet after 20 Jul 2026. Same
   number feeds the homepage case-study result line (commented out in index.html).
2. **[two] / [twenty]**: only say capacity numbers Isaiah will honour. One a month is
   fine to say; it sounds more like a bloke and less like a funnel.
3. The reactivation approval line ("checks with you before anything sends") and the
   instant-answer flow must match how a live instance actually behaves before this ships,
   not the design doc. If reactivation is not live in an instance yet, soften to "it's
   built to" or cut the sentence rather than claim it.

## Production notes

- Phone camera at eye level, one take per beat so it stays loose. Rough edges are the brand.
- Screen recordings straight off the real system (site form, reply email, Telegram buzz).
- No music, no logo animation.
